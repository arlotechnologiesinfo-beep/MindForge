# MindForge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

MindForge is an offline brain-training app. **It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- The app has no INTERNET permission and cannot send data off your device.
- Profiles, game progress, scores, achievements, and settings are stored only locally on your device.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

MindForge is a recreational app for general audiences. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

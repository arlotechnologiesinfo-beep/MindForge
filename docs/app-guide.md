# MindForge — App Guide

Offline brain-training app for Android. 62 short games in 10 categories, free, no ads, no purchases, no account, no internet permission. Progress is stored only on the device.

How a game works: each game is 8 rounds. Every game has 10 difficulty levels that adapt to how you play (strong results move the level up, weak results move it down). A session gives a score, XP, and may unlock achievements.

## 2. Screens

Bottom navigation has four tabs: Home, Games, Progress, Profile. Game and Result screens open full screen without the tab bar.

### Home
- Greeting ("Hello, <name>").
- Rank card: current rank, total XP, day streak, progress bar and XP needed for the next rank.
- **Today's Brain Mix**: 5 games chosen by date, each with a Play button.
- Big Start button for the first game of the mix.
- Suggestion card: names your lowest recent category and offers a game to try.

### Games
- Title shows the game count (62 games).
- Category chips (All, Memory, Focus, Logic, Math, Words, Spatial, Speed, Strategy, Problem Solving, Visual) to filter the list.
- Each row: game name, one-line description, your current level and best score. Tap to play.

### Play (a game)
- Header: back arrow, game name, current level.
- Intro card: category, name, description, **Start** button.
- Top progress bar for rounds, countdown timer bar per round.
- Round types: multiple choice, typed number, grid recall, sequence pads, odd-one-out grid.
- Instant feedback: "Correct" or "Not quite", with optional vibration on wrong answers.
- Time running out counts as a wrong answer.

### Result
- Score, "New best!" when beaten.
- Stats: correct of 8, accuracy, average time, best streak, XP earned.
- "Next time: level N" from the adaptive difficulty.
- Achievement card when something unlocks.
- Buttons: **Play again**, **Done**.

### Progress
- Rank card (rank, XP, streak).
- **Game performance**: recent accuracy per category ("Not played" until you play one). Footnote: not a medical or IQ measure.
- **Achievements** list (16), locked and unlocked.

### Profile
- **Players on this device**: select, Rename, Delete (when more than one), Add player. Each player has separate progress.
- **Settings**: Large text, Reduce motion, Vibrate on wrong answers.
- **Privacy**: works fully offline, no accounts, ads or internet access; data stays on the device and is removed on uninstall. Disclaimer: games are for practice and fun, not diagnosis or treatment.
